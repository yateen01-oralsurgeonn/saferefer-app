# #BuildHybridAI 2026 Rules

## Deadline
- 30 Nov 2026, 2:00 PM PT = **1 Dec 2026, 3:30 AM IST**
- Judging 10–16 Dec; winners about 21 Dec

## Eligibility
- India allowed. Excluded: Brazil, Italy, Quebec, Crimea, Russia, Syria, Sudan, Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, OFAC-sanctioned countries
- Age of majority in country of residence
- Solo allowed. Team size: not stated

## Required technology
- Firebase AI Logic with App Check (debug tokens accepted)
- One platform: Web/Chrome (Gemini Nano 4), Android (Gemini Nano 4) or iOS (Apple Foundation Models)
- Hybrid: on-device first, cloud fallback
- Encouraged: Remote Config, Firestore, Auth. Multi-platform favoured for Grand Prize

## Judging
- Stage 1: pass/fail viability
- Stage 2: four equal criteria, scored 1–5: technological implementation, design, potential impact, quality of idea
- Percentages: not stated (equal)

## Submission
- Demo video: max 3 min, public on YouTube, English or subtitles
- Public repo: full source, instructions, visible open-source licence, Firebase AI Logic called at runtime
- No production keys, secrets or PII
- Free judge access until judging ends
- Text description: Firebase AI Logic use, features, value
- Disclose pre-existing code
- Hosted URL optional; no changes after deadline

## IP
- You own the project and video
- Google gets a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence for judging and promotion, plus your name and likeness

## Prizes ($30,000)
- Grand $10,000; 1st runner-up $7,000; 2nd runner-up $4,000
- Best iOS, Android, Web/Chrome: $3,000 each
